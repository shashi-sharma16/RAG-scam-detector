Phishing scams are attempts to trick people into revealing sensitive information such as passwords, banking credentials, OTPs, or credit card information. Phishing messages often create a sense of urgency and may contain suspicious links.

A common warning sign of a phishing message is a request to click an unfamiliar link. Attackers may use messages claiming that an account will be blocked or suspended unless the recipient takes immediate action.

Fake job scams often promise unusually high salaries for simple work. They may ask applicants to pay registration fees, training fees, security deposits, or other charges before receiving employment.

A legitimate employer generally does not require applicants to pay money in order to receive a job offer. Requests for payment during recruitment should be treated as a warning sign.

Investment scams may promise guaranteed or unusually high returns with little or no risk. Scammers may pressure victims to invest quickly or transfer money to personal accounts.

People should verify financial or employment claims using official sources before sending money or sharing sensitive information.
