ABOUT RAG SENTINEL

RAG Sentinel is a local scam-awareness project that combines semantic retrieval with language-model analysis. It retrieves relevant information from a scam knowledge base and uses that context to explain potential warning signs and safety actions. The quality of its results depends on the available knowledge, retrieval quality, and analysis logic. Always verify important claims through trusted official channels.
